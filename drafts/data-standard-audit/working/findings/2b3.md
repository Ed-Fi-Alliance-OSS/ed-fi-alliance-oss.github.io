# Findings: 2b3 (Student Attendance, Special Education Data Model, Graduation, Path)

## Scope

Rubric audit of the Ed-Fi Data Standard v6 model-reference pages in four domain folders: `student-attendance-domain` (8 pages incl. 4 best-practices pages, plus 2 .mmd), `special-education-data-model-domain` (4 pages + 2 .mmd), `graduation-domain` (4 pages + 2 .mmd), and `path-domain` (4 pages + 2 .mmd). All paths are under `dataStandard_versioned_docs/version-6/model-reference/`. Questions in scope: the 8 each for attendance, graduation, path and special education in `questions.yaml` (32 total).

## Method

- Read every source page and .mmd, and the built HTML for diagrams, readme, and one best-practices page (`build/reference/data-exchange/data-standard/model-reference/...`); used `inventory-html.csv`, `version-diff.csv`, `table-dupes.csv`, `terminology-hits.csv`.
- Scored each page type per domain against the rubric (weights from the rubric table; criteria not present on a page are shown as "n/a (not present)" and left out of the weighted mean).
- Answerability judged only from published prose and tables; diagram-only answers are counted as gaps. Entity lists in .mmd files were compared with overview text and the entities tables.
- AI-assisted analysis: scores are judgments to be reviewed by a person; evidence quotes are from source files.

## Summary

- Diagram pages are text-empty in built HTML (25 to 28 visible words on SA, GR, PA) and none states a relationship in words (R1 = 0); SEDM adds only the admonition.
- Three of the four overviews (SA, GR, PA) open directly with `## Key Entities`, have no links, no version, and an auto-description of literally "Key Entities".
- Student Attendance best-practices is the strongest content in scope (use-case table, patterns, per-entity rules) but has 5+ typos, bare "Ed-Fi" in 11 lines, and `SectionAttendanceEventTaken` as a variant name; the overview misnames the entity and calls the AttendanceEvent common an entity.
- Overview, BP index, entities table and diagram disagree on Intervention attendance (StudentInterventionAttendanceEvent belongs to the Intervention domain) and on `StudentProgramAssociation` vs `GeneralStudentProgramAssociation`.
- Entities tables mix inherited and owned descriptors: SA owns 1 of 39 descriptors, GR 9 of 51; PA and SEDM are clean. Four entities pages have 0.31 to 0.51 empty cells and no lead-in.
- SEDM is the best exemplar for early-access lifecycle (V4) and the "Differences from StudentSpecialEducationProgramAssociation" comparison table, but has the "Individual Eduction Program" typo, unexpanded acronyms (PCG, AEM, LEA, SEA), unsourced statistics, and a ServiceProviderType description copy error.
- Path pages have the most naming drift (CompletionIndicators, "Status Events", Candidate/Person, "Path Name") and an unstated scope (educator candidates vs any student); Path overview has 4 grammar errors.
- No page in the four domains links to What's New (0 matches), even though Path (v6.0) and SEDM (v6.1) are new; q-path-08 is answerable only on What's New pages.
- Answerability: of 32 questions, 21 full, 10 partial, 1 none (q-grad-08). Weak spots are vocabulary mismatches: "end-of-course exams", "district-wide", "career pathways", "days absent", "IEP goal progress".
- Lowest weighted page scores: SA overview 1.2 and diagrams 1.2 (all domains' diagrams 1.2 to 1.6); highest: SEDM readme and overview 1.9, GR readme 1.9.
