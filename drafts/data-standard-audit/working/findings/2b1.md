# Agent 2b1 Findings: Alternative and Supplemental Services, Education Organization, Performance Evaluation (Data Standard v6)

AI-assisted: drafted by an AI agent; a person must review before use.

## Scope

All published v6 pages under `dataStandard_versioned_docs/version-6/model-reference/` for three domains, plus their `.mmd` diagram sources:

- `alternative-and-supplemental-services-domain/` (readme, overview, model-diagrams, entities-references-and-descriptors, best-practices, 4 `.mmd`)
- `education-organization-domain/` (readme, overview, model-diagrams, entities-and-descriptors, 2 `.mmd`; no best-practices page exists)
- `performance-evaluation-domain/` (readme, overview, model-diagrams, entities-references-and-descriptors, 2 `.mmd`; no best-practices page exists)

## Method

- Read every source page against the rubric criteria for its page type; cross-checked `inventory-html.csv`, `version-diff.csv`, `table-dupes.csv`, `terminology-hits.csv` and `retrieval-results.csv`.
- Looked at built HTML under `build/reference/data-exchange/data-standard/model-reference/<domain>/` for `<title>`, meta description, breadcrumb and version text.
- Extracted entity names from the `.mmd` sources and compared them with overview and entities-table entities.
- Counted descriptor-table rows whose `Entity` column belongs to the domain (domain-owned) versus inherited.
- Answered each domain question using only the published pages (see Answerability).
- Scores: integers 0-3, `n/a` where the page-type table says not applicable or the page has no such content (for example, no tables). Score is the lower of the new-learner and answer-engine views.

## Summary

- Education Organization overview is the strongest of the three on explanation (the "EducationOrganization Abstract Type" section answers q-edorg-02 and q-edorg-03 in one contiguous passage), but it still says "Student, Parent, and Staff" in a v6 page (Contact replaced Parent in v5) and uses bare "Ed-Fi data model" five times.
- Performance Evaluation overview lists only 4 of the 13 entities in its Key Entities section. All five `*Rating` entities, `RubricDimension`, `QuantitativeMeasure*`, `SurveySectionAggregateResponse` and `Goal` appear only in the entities table, so "how do I record a teacher observation rating" is answerable only by inference.
- Alternative and Supplemental Services best-practices (4,791 words) has the richest guidance of the batch, but it has corrupted attribute names in key tables (`EntrEducationOrganization Date (key)`, `Staf (key)`, `GeneralStudentProgamAssociation` x4), a dangling sentence, 1 link in 4,791 words, and stale attribute names (`LastEvaluationDate`, renamed `IEPLastEvaluationDate` in v6).
- Entity inventories disagree across overview, table and diagram in every domain. Examples: `StudentSection504ProgramAssociation` is in the diagram and best-practices but not the entities table; the Education Organization overview omits 3 of 16 table entities; the Performance Evaluation diagrams omit `RubricDimension`.
- 44 of 77 descriptor rows (57%) on the Alternative and Supplemental Services entities page are inherited from Staff, Student, School, Section and EducationOrganization with no labeling (T3 = 0). Education Organization and Performance Evaluation are clean (34/35 and 17/17 domain-owned).
- Education Organization and Performance Evaluation have no best-practices page. Education Organization is the most reused entity in the model (the `EducationOrganization` extended reference appears in 21 other domains per `table-dupes.csv`), and questions about SEA optionality and charter management organizations (CMOs) are only partly answerable.
- Built-HTML check: the meta description of each page is just the first H2 or first sentence ("Key Entities", "Definitions and Key Concepts", "Education Organization Model UML Diagram"). The only version signal a crawler sees is nav chrome ("Unifying Data Model - v6 Model Reference", "Version: 6"), not the article body.
- Learner vocabulary that never appears in these domains: "after-school", "clubs", "tutoring", "professional growth", "Danielson", "regional service center", "CMO". "Observation" appears only in the Performance Evaluation readme.
- Wrong-version retrieval is likely: `retrieval-results.csv` shows v5/v4/v3 pages as top-1 for q-edorg-03, q-edorg-06, q-edorg-07 and q-altsvc-08.
