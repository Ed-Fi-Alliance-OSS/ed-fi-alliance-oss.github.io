# Agent 2a2: Mechanical quality scan (v5 and UDM)

AI-assisted scan. Review by a person before any finding is acted on.

## Scope

- 119 pages: 109 Data Standard v5 pages (`dataStandard_versioned_docs/version-5/`, inventory `set=v5`) and 10 UDM pages (`docs/reference/1-data-exchange/udm/` and `docs/reference/1-data-exchange/data-standards.md`, inventory `set=udm`).
- Read-only on `docs/`, `dataStandard_versioned_docs/`, and `build/`. Scratch scripts are in `/tmp/claude-0/2a2/`.

## Method

- Repeated words (per line and across line breaks), placeholder terms (TODO, TBD, Coming Soon, and similar), and "Go back" text: regex over source with fenced code stripped.
- Typos: words that appear only in the v5 and UDM set (1 to 2 times) and nowhere else in the repo's docs or blog, reviewed by hand, plus a list of common misspellings.
- Headings: H1 count per file, level skips, empty sections, repeated heading text across the 119 pages, and whether the H1 is followed by prose.
- Links: every relative Markdown link and image resolved against the source file (`.md`, `.mdx`, `readme.md`, and `index.md` variants, plus anchors). Absolute site routes checked for existence in `build/`.
- Bullets and emphasis: counted `*`, `-`, `_`, and `**` markers in source. Ran `node_modules/.bin/markdownlint-cli2` (v0.23.3) with the repo's `.markdownlint.yaml` on the in-scope files.
- Tables: per-column empty-cell ratio for every Markdown table, and a closer look at the entities tables.
- Dates and support statements: grep for years, school years, "latest", "current", "upcoming", and "will be", checked against today (2026-10-10).

## Summary

- Two H findings, both on `docs/reference/1-data-exchange/data-standards.md`: the "Active Versions" section still presents Data Standard 4.0 as supporting 2024-2025 and 2025-2026, and presents 3.x as having 2024-2025 still ahead ("will be the last year"). Both dates are past as of 2026-10-10.
- Entities tables are the largest structural problem: in 16 of the 20 classification tables, the EDFacts Mapping column is at least 90% empty, and in 13 tables EDFacts Mapping, Commonly Used, and Commonly State-Defined are all at least 90% empty (domain-template).
- 77 of 119 pages have no prose between the H1 and the first heading or table (for example, `## Key Entities`). This is the largest human-orientation gap (H1 = 0), and 20 of 21 domain-overview pages are affected.
- The 21 domain readmes are link hubs: a one-line intro followed by 3 to 6 links. Link text is 17% to 71% of visible words. Twenty of 22 domain readmes also end with a "Go back" line, with two different labels ("UDM Reference" and "Model Reference").
- Generic headings repeat across pages: "Key Concepts" (22 pages), "Key Entities" (21), "Extended References" (20), and "Subsections" (4). None are distinguishable out of context.
- Typos and grammar: about 14 single-word errors (including a misspelled entity name, `GeneralStudentProgamAssociation`, in four places on the Alternative and Supplemental Services best-practices page, and a garbled key name, `EntrEducationOrganization Date`). None are placeholders.
- Links: all 80 relative links resolve; 14 absolute site routes resolve in `build/`; 274 external links were not fetched. One broken image link exists, inside an HTML comment, so it does not render.
- Bullet style: 1,292 bullets use `*` and none use `-`, which contradicts CLAUDE.md. The repo lint rule only requires consistency within a file (`ul-style: consistent`), so these pass lint.
- Placeholders: none. No empty sections. Exactly one H1 per page. Two pages skip H2 to H4.
