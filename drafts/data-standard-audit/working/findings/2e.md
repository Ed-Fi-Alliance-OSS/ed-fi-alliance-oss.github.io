# 2e: Cross-Version Redundancy and Retirement Analysis (Ed-Fi Data Standard v3–v6)

## Scope

The `dataStandard` docs plugin instance (`dataStandard_versioned_docs/version-3`
through `version-6`, routed at `/reference/data-exchange/data-standard[/N]`),
plus the pages that describe version status and pairing:
`docs/reference/0-roadmap/supported-versions.md`,
`docs/reference/1-data-exchange/data-standards.md`,
`docs/reference/1-data-exchange/readme.md`,
`docs/reference/1-data-exchange/versioning-and-releases.md`, the v4/v5
certifications under `docs/partners/2-certification/`, the version dropdown
component, and the Docusaurus config. Status is assessed as of 2026-10-10.
I assume a school year ends on June 30.

## Method

- Similarity: `working/version-diff.csv` (v6 compared with each older version
  per logical page). I also ran a scratch pairwise `difflib` ratio for v3↔v4,
  v4↔v5, and v3↔v5, because the CSV only compares against v6.
- Retrieval: `working/retrieval-results.csv` (251 questions), several ad hoc
  `scripts/retrieval.py` queries, and a scratch counterfactual that re-indexes
  `corpus.jsonl` with versions removed. The counterfactual simulates retirement
  or `noindex`. It used the same BM25 parameters as `retrieval.py`. Treat the
  results as directional only.
- Built HTML: `<title>`, canonical, robots, the version banner, and redirect
  stubs under `build/`; URL counts in `build/sitemap.xml`.
- Config: `docusaurus.config.js`, `dataStandard_versions.json`, `src/theme/*`,
  `src/components/VersionDropDown`, and the installed Docusaurus 3.10.2 option
  schemas (`node_modules/@docusaurus/*`), which I checked for supported
  options.
- Inbound links: I grepped `docs/`, `blog/`, `src/`, `odsApi_versioned_docs/`,
  and `dataStandard_versioned_docs/` for `data-standard/3` and
  `data-standard/4`, and for relative `version-3`/`version-4` links.
- Certifications: `certificationData.js` `validThrough` dates for the v4
  listings.

## Summary

- **Most of v3–v5 is a copy.** Among pages that have a v6 counterpart, 28 of
  65 (v3), 36 of 68 (v4), and 49 of 80 (v5) are at least 0.98 similar to v6.
  The older versions are even closer to _each other_: v4↔v5 has 75 of 90
  shared pages at 0.98 or higher, and 67 are byte-identical. The only content
  that really differs by version is the entity/descriptor tables (median
  similarity to v6 is about 0.5) and the What's New pages.
- **Older versions win retrieval.** An older version is in the top 3 for 174
  of 251 questions (69%), and ranks first for 63. For 75 questions, an
  older-version _twin_ of the expected v6 page is in the top 3. In 23 of those
  it ranks first. v5 accounts for 54 of the 75. v6 entity pages are a median
  1.65× longer than v5 ones, so BM25-style length normalization favors the
  shorter old tables.
- **Retiring v3 and v4 alone is not enough for Perplexity.** In the
  counterfactual, dropping v3 and v4 raises hit@3 from 42% to 51%, but an older
  version is still in the top 3 for 67% of questions, because v5 fills the
  gap. Also canonicalizing or hiding the 49 near-identical v5 pages cuts that
  to 45%. Dropping all old versions gives hit@3 56%.
- **Support status is stale on the site.** Per `supported-versions.md`, DS
  3.3.1 ended after 2024-2025 and DS 4.0.0 after 2025-2026, so both are now
  out of support. Even so, the site labels v4 "active" or "Supported" in 4
  places: the config `className`, the banner text, the dropdown, and two
  landing pages. `data-standards.md` still lists v3 under "Active Versions".
- **The v4 docs are not reliable DS 4.0 documentation.** The v4 Student
  Identification pages list the `Contact` entity and `StudentContactAssociation`.
  Those came from the Parent→Contact rename, which the v5 What's New page
  credits to DS 5.0. Descriptor rows on the same v4 page still say `Parent`.
- **DS 4 still has live dependents, but none link to the v4 docs.** ODS/API
  7.3, supported through 2028-2029, still ships DS 4.0.0 images. Ed-Fi
  OneRoster supports DS 4.0. SIS v4 and Domain API v4 certifications are still
  marked "Status: Active". Two SIS v4 listings are valid until 2026-12-12 and
  2027-06-05. No certification page links into `/data-standard/4/`. The 15
  inbound links to `/3` and `/4` all come from landing pages, older ODS/API
  docs, and the version dropdown.
- **Recommendation:**
  - **v3:** retire now, by removing it from the build and redirecting its
    pages.
  - **v4:** apply `noIndex` and an "unmaintained" banner now. Retire it on
    2027-07-01, or sooner if the Data Standard and ODS/API owners agree.
  - **v5:** keep it but deprioritize it, using version-labeled titles and
    canonicals to v6 for the 49 near-identical pages. Plan `noIndex` for
    2027-2028.
- **Docusaurus 3.10 supports per-version `noIndex: true`.** It emits
  `noindex, nofollow` and drops those URLs from the sitemap, so you don't need
  a theme wrapper or `ignorePatterns`. Redirects on GitHub Pages are
  `plugin-client-redirects` meta-refresh stubs (no HTTP 301). The
  broken-link checker does not treat redirect stubs as valid targets, so every
  inbound link must be updated before removal.
- Findings: 16 (5 H, 10 M, 1 L).

## Evidence Summary

### Duplication with v6 (from `version-diff.csv`)

| Version | Pages | Has v6 counterpart | ≥ 0.95 | ≥ 0.98 | Identical (1.0) | < 0.5 | Median sim |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| v3 | 88 | 65 | 32 | 28 | 13 | 14 | 0.928 |
| v4 | 92 | 68 | 37 | 36 | 19 | 13 | 0.986 |
| v5 | 109 | 80 | 50 | 49 | 27 | 11 | 0.989 |

- The CSV reports 23, 24, and 29 pages as having no v6 equivalent, but most of
  these are not real gaps. The model-diagrams pages changed from `.md` to `.mdx`
  in v6, and the URL is unchanged. Checking against built URLs, only 6 (v3),
  7 (v4), and 9 (v5) URLs truly have no v6 path. They are:
  - the `educator-preparation-data-model-domain/*` pages, which were renamed to
    `educator-preparation-program-domain` and `path-domain` in v6
  - the What's New pages for that version
- Entity/descriptor pages are the real version-specific content. Their median
  similarity to v6 is 0.50 in every older version, and none reach 0.98. Among
  the older versions, the v4↔v5 entities median is 0.998 and the v3↔v4 median
  is 0.986. In other words, v3–v5 tables barely differ from each other.

### Retrieval (from `retrieval-results.csv`, 251 questions)

- Top-1 version: v6 101, v5 33, v3 19, v4 11, non-Data-Standard page 87.
- `older_version_in_top3 = True`: 174 of 251 (69%).
- Older twin of an expected page in the top 3: 75 questions. By version, v5
  has 54, v3 has 12, and v4 has 9. The twin is at rank 1 in 23.
- Ad hoc example: for "Ed-Fi Data Standard graduation plan entity", the top
  results are:
  - rank 1: an SIS v5 certification scenario page
  - ranks 2–4: the v3, v4, and v5 `graduation-domain/entities-references-and-descriptors`
    pages
  - rank 9: the first v6 page (the overview)

Counterfactual (scratch re-index of `corpus.jsonl`; same BM25 settings):

| Scenario | Docs | hit@1 | hit@3 | Older version ranked 1 | Older version in top 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Current | 1754 | 25% | 42% | 25% | 69% |
| Without v3 | 1675 | 27% | 47% | 22% | 68% |
| Without v3, v4 | 1592 | 29% | 51% | 19% | 67% |
| Without v3, v4, plus 49 near-identical v5 pages canonicalized | 1543 | 29% | 52% | 18% | 45% |
| Without v3, v4, v5 | 1492 | 36% | 56% | 0% | 0% |

In the canonicalized scenario, the v5 pages that remain in the top 3 are
mostly entity tables (61 slots) and What's New pages (20).

## Recommendations per Version

### v3: Retire (remove from build, redirect, archive outside the site)

- It is out of support (2024-2025 was the last school year), no certification
  is issued for it, and the paired ODS/API 5.x is out of support.
- Only 28 of 65 matched pages are ≥ 0.98 to v6. However, the version-specific
  tables are also near-identical to v4 (v3↔v4 entities median 0.986), so they
  add little unique value.
- In retrieval it is the top-1 result for 19 questions and the twin-outranker
  in 12.
- Losing the docs costs little:
  - The normative v3.3 detail already lives outside the site, in the UDM
    Handbook at `edfidocs.blob.core.windows.net/$web/handbook/v3.3/` and in
    the Ed-Fi-Standard GitHub releases.
  - The pages have 4 inbound links in total (the dropdown, `data-standards.md`,
    `migration-utility.md`, and the ODS/API 5.4 bulk-load how-to).
- Certification impact: none on links. The certification registry links to
  `/reference/data-exchange/data-standards/#version-3x`, so keep that heading
  (or its anchor) when you rewrite `data-standards.md`.
- Archive: tag the repo before deletion (for example, `ds3-docs-final`) so the
  Markdown stays viewable on GitHub. Link the v3.3 handbook from a "Retired
  versions" section of `data-standards.md`.

### v4: Keep-but-deprioritize now, then retire on 2027-07-01 (or sooner)

- **Arguments for retiring now:**
  - Support ended after the 2025-2026 school year.
  - 36 of 68 matched pages are ≥ 0.98 to v6, and 67 of 90 pages are
    byte-identical to v5.
  - The v4 docs contain DS 5.0 content (`Contact`, `StudentContactAssociation`;
    see 2e-006), so they can give wrong DS 4.0 answers.
  - No certification page links into them.
- **Arguments for not removing it today:**
  - The site itself says ODS/API 7.3 supports DS 4.0.0 through 2028-2029.
  - Ed-Fi OneRoster supports DS 4.0.
  - SIS v4 and Domain API v4 certifications are still "Status: Active", with
    a listing valid until 2027-06-05.
  - Removing the docs without a product statement would contradict those pages.
- **Plan:**
  - Now: `noIndex: true`, the `unmaintained` banner, and corrected status labels
    everywhere.
  - Then: retire after the last SIS v4 listing expires and the 2026-2027
    school year ends (2027-07-01).
  - Retire sooner if the Data Standard and ODS/API owners confirm that DS 4.0
    on ODS/API 7.3 is "runs, but not documented here".
- Redirect target: send v4 to v6 (same rule as v3) rather than v5. This avoids
  a v4→v5→v6 chain when v5 retires. It also avoids strengthening v5, which is
  the biggest retrieval competitor.
- The DS 4.0 handbook (`handbook/v4.0/`) remains the archive for detail.

### v5: Keep, and deprioritize for answer engines

- It is supported through 2027-2028, paired with ODS/API 7.3 and Ed-Fi API
  8.0/8.1, and the v5 certifications are current. It must stay published and
  findable for "Data Standard 5" questions.
- It is also the main source of wrong-version retrieval: 54 of 75 twin cases.
  Recommended measures:
  1. Add the version to `<title>` and to a visible line above the H1 for all
     versions (see Option F).
  2. Point the canonical to v6 _only_ for the 49 pages ≥ 0.98 similar to v6
     (mostly overview, readme, and best-practices pages). Keep entity tables
     and What's New self-canonical, because they carry real v5-specific
     content.
  3. Schedule `noIndex: true` for v5 at the start of the 2027-2028 school
     year, and plan retirement after 2028-06-30.
- Do not block v5 for PerplexityBot now. That would make DS 5 questions
  unanswerable while v5 is supported.

## Implementation Options (Docusaurus 3.10.2) and What Each Does for Perplexity

Hosting note: the site deploys to GitHub Pages
(`.github/workflows/on-merge-main.yml`, `actions/deploy-pages`), so there are
no server-side 301s. `@docusaurus/plugin-client-redirects` writes a stub
`index.html` at each `from` path. Each stub contains an instant
`<meta http-equiv="refresh">`, a JS redirect, and a _relative_
`<link rel="canonical">`. See
`build/reference/admin-app/system-administrators/index.html`.

Proposed redirect rule (add before `return undefined;` in `createRedirects`):

```js
const DS = '/reference/data-exchange/data-standard';
const RETIRED_DS = ['3']; // add '4' at v4 retirement
// v6 is the unversioned route; skip versioned (/5/...) paths and the root.
if (
  existingPath.startsWith(`${DS}/`) &&
  existingPath !== `${DS}/` &&
  !/^\/reference\/data-exchange\/data-standard\/\d+(\/|$)/.test(existingPath)
) {
  return RETIRED_DS.map((v) => existingPath.replace(`${DS}/`, `${DS}/${v}/`));
}
```

This maps `/3/<path>` to `/<path>` wherever v6 has the same slug, including
`model-diagrams` (`.md` became `.mdx`, same slug) and
`education-organization-domain/entities-and-descriptors`. These exceptions
need explicit `redirects` entries:

| From (v3, and later v4) | To | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |

| `/3/handbook` | `/reference/data-exchange/data-standards` (retired-versions section) | The rule would send them to the **v6** handbook, which is the wrong artifact. Link `handbook/v3.3/` from that section. |
| `/3/whats-new`, `/3/whats-new/whats-new-v33-a`, `/3/whats-new/whats-new-v33-b` | `/reference/data-exchange/data-standards` | There is no v6 equivalent. v6 What's New covers 6.x only. |

## Risks and Sequencing

1. **Decide and communicate** (before any code change):
   - The Data Standard team and ODS/API product owners need to agree on the
     DS 4.0 story for ODS/API 7.3 and OneRoster.
   - The certification team needs to confirm v4 certification status. Change
     SIS v4 and Domain API v4 from "Status: Active" to a sunset date.
   - Publish a short blog post or roadmap notification, matching the existing
     `0-roadmap/notifications/` pattern, giving the retirement dates and the
     archive locations.
2. **Correct status text** (S effort, no structural risk):
   - `supported-versions.md`, `data-standards.md` (move v3 and v4 under
     "Retired versions" but keep the `version-3x` anchor), and
     `1-data-exchange/readme.md` (remove "(active)" from v4).
   - `VersionDropDown` labels.
   - The v4 `className` and `banner`, and the v3 banner text (Option I).
3. **Hide v4** with `noIndex: true` (Option C). Fix 2e-006 in place only if v4
   is to stay public for long; otherwise just add a note saying the entity
   tables match DS 5.
4. **Add version titles** (Option F) across all versions. This helps whatever
   is still indexed.
5. **Retire v3:**
   1. Update the build-checked inbound links: the `VersionDropDown` entries,
      `data-standards.md`, `migration-utility.md`, and
      `odsApi_versioned_docs/version-5.4/.../bulk-load-client-utility.mdx`.
   2. Apply Option A.
   3. Apply Option B, the rule plus the exceptions.
   4. Run `npm run build`.
6. **v5 canonicals** (Option E) with an allowlist generated from
   `version-diff.csv` (`sim_v6_v5 >= 0.98`). Re-check before each Data
   Standard release.
7. **Retire v4 on 2027-07-01:**
   1. Update the 6 build-checked v4 links in `odsApi_versioned_docs/version-6.2`
      and `docs/`, plus the dropdown.
   2. Update the 4 absolute links in `odsApi_versioned_docs/version-7.1`.
      These won't fail the build, but they will rely on redirects.
   3. Add `'4'` to `RETIRED_DS`.
8. **v5:** apply `noIndex` around 2027-08 and retire after 2028-06-30, using
   the same steps.

Risks:

- Readers redirected from v3 or v4 land on v6 entity tables that differ
  (similarity ≈ 0.5). Without a visible note on v6 pages ("Looking for a
  retired version? See …"), they may assume v6 tables apply to their DS 4.0
  system.
- The client-redirects plugin silently skips a `from` path that already
  exists. Leftover files from a partial removal would leave live old pages
  instead of stubs.
- Perplexity's index refresh is outside our control. Old URLs may keep being
  cited for weeks. Plan a re-test using `questions.yaml` against live
  Perplexity after deployment.
- `onBrokenAnchors` defaults to `warn`, so removing the `Version 3.x` heading
  in `data-standards.md` would silently break the certification registry
  links.
- Algolia (`contextualSearch: false`) keeps returning cached v3/v4 records
  until the crawler reruns.

Verify with `npm run build`:

- There are no broken links, and no redirect-collision warnings.
- Redirect stubs exist for every retired URL.
- `build/sitemap.xml` has no `/data-standard/3/` URLs (or `/4/` once `noIndex`
  is applied).
- v4 pages contain `noindex, nofollow`.
- Each page has exactly one `<link rel="canonical">`.
- `<title>` includes the version on a sample of pages from each version.
