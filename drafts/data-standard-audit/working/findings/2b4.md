# Audit findings 2b4: Enrollment, Staff, Student Cohort, Credential domains (Data Standard v6)

AI-assisted: drafted with an AI model; scores and findings need human review before use.

## Scope

All published v6 pages under `dataStandard_versioned_docs/version-6/model-reference/` for four domains: `enrollment-domain` (readme, overview, model-diagrams, entities, best-practices, 2 `.mmd`), `staff-domain` (readme, overview, model-diagrams, entities, 2 `.mmd`), `student-cohort-domain` (readme, overview, model-diagrams, entities, 2 `.mmd`), `credential-domain` (readme, overview, model-diagrams, entities, 2 `.mmd`). That is 19 pages plus 8 `.mmd` sources. There is no best-practices page for Staff, Student Cohort or Credential. Questions: 32 (`q-enroll-*`, `q-staff-*`, `q-cohort-*`, `q-cred-*`).

## Method

Read every source page and `.mmd`; compared against `build/.../index.html` (title, meta description, article text); used `inventory-html.csv`, `version-diff.csv`, `terminology-hits.csv`, and `retrieval-results.csv`. Computed descriptor-table ownership and blank-cell counts with a script over the entities tables. Scored per the rubric (weights from the page-type table; `n/a` where the page has no tables or images or the rubric marks the criterion "–"). Answerability used only published pages (domain pages plus What's New where they hold the answer). Pages' `<title>` and meta description checked in `build/`.

## Summary

- All four diagram pages are text-empty to a crawler: 25–28 visible words, `mermaid_containers` = 0, and the `.mmd` relationship labels are almost all `"HasAssociated"`, so even the download carries little prose meaning. Highest-impact fix: a crawlable "Entities and relationships" list per diagram (finding 2b4-01).
- Credential is the weakest domain: the readme is boilerplate ("provides background, rationale, and provides guidance"), the overview names only 2 of 5 entities (CertificationExam, CertificationExamResult and CredentialEvent appear only in the entities table), and nothing on any Credential page says the domain is new in v6.0 (2b4-12 to 2b4-16).
- Enrollment overview contradicts its own entities table and diagram: it says "The StudentEducationOrganizationAssociation is not a part of this domain" while that entity is a row in the table and a node in `Enrollment.mmd`. It also omits CrisisEvent and StudentTransportation, which the best-practices page treats as core (2b4-04, 2b4-06).
- Verified: `StudentDemographic` in the Enrollment overview is _not_ a stale or retired name. It is a real v6 entity (Migration Guide `/studentDemographics`), but it is listed as a key entity in both Enrollment and Student Identification and Demographics, with no ownership explanation (2b4-05).
- Enrollment best practices is the strongest page in the batch (use-case table, anti-patterns, in-page "Changes in Data Standard v6.1" note) but scores low on Q1 (about six typos and a split bullet), G1 (7 bare "Ed-Fi" uses), and stale EPDM wording (2b4-09, 2b4-10, 2b4-14).
- Student Cohort overview is byte-identical to v3/v4/v5 (`sim_v6_v5` = 1.0) and has zero links; retrieval for "cohort vs section vs program" returns a v4 page first (2b4-26, 2b4-27). There is no guidance on cohort versus program (q-cohort-08 = none).
- Staff overview is the best overview of the four (explains the 6.0 StaffDirectory change and employment versus assignment), but `OpenStaffPositionEvent` descriptors exist with no entity row, diagram node, or overview mention (2b4-19), and the learner words "email", "phone", "state educator ID" and "teacher works at two schools" never appear (2b4-22).
- Entities pages: empty-cell ratios 0.435 (Enrollment), 0.347 (Staff), 0.343 (Student Cohort), 0.386 (Credential); in Student Cohort only 3 of 42 descriptor rows belong to a domain-owned entity (2b4-16, 2b4-29).
- Answerability across 32 questions: 15 full, 16 partial, 1 none (one of the partials, q-cred-06, is answered only outside the domain); see the Answerability table.
