## Scope

four domains, plus their `.mmd` sources: `student-academic-record-domain` (5
pages, 8 `.mmd`), `finance-domain` (5 pages, 2 `.mmd`), `survey-domain` (4

these four.

## Method

- Read all source pages and `.mmd` files in full, scored each page against the
  rubric criteria for its page type (weights from `rubric.md`).
- Checked built HTML under `build/reference/data-exchange/data-standard/model-reference/<domain>/`
  for `<title>`, meta description, canonical, and visible text.
- Used `inventory-html.csv`, `version-diff.csv`, `table-dupes.csv`, and
  `terminology-hits.csv` for metrics; `grep` across `dataStandard_versioned_docs/version-6`
  and `docs/` for cross-page checks.
- Answerability uses published pages only. Entity-list consistency compared
  the `.mmd` node lists, the overview prose, and the entities tables.
- Scores in the score tables are 0-3. `n/a` = the rubric marks the criterion
  not applicable for that page type. `n/a*` = rubric weight exists but the page
  has no table, image, link, or diagram to judge, so it was excluded from the
  weighted mean. The M2 = 0 and V1 = 1 scores are the known site-wide state.

## Summary

- Recruiting and Staffing overview is a placeholder: both sections read
  "_Coming Soon_" (verified in source and built HTML, 2 occurrences). The page
  is 19 source words. Every Recruiting question about concepts (q-recruit-06,
  q-recruit-07) gets no answer. Lowest weighted score in the batch (0.9).
- Recruiting naming splits three ways: H1 "Recruitment And Staffing", slug and
  What's New "Recruiting and Staffing" or "RecruitingAndStaffing", and the UDM
  Ed-Fi Domains page does not list the domain at all.
- Survey model-diagrams page says "reference information for the _Assessment_
  domain model", and that sentence becomes the page's meta description in built
  HTML. Survey overview uses the retired name "Parent" and omits the person
  target associations that What's New lists as added in 6.0.
- Survey is the weakest fully-populated domain (overview 1.1, diagrams 1.2).
  Finance is the most substantive (best-practices has JSON examples, SEA/LEA/CMO
  use cases) but stale: it frames itself as "Data Standard v4.0-a" and uses
  inconsistent names (`ChartOfAccounts` vs `ChartOfAccount`, "Account entity",
  "CMP").
- Student Academic Record best practices are strong on how-to content (all 8
  questions answered at least partly, 6 fully) but the GPA, AcademicHonor, and Recognition
  structures appear only in a mapping table or in `.mmd` text; AcademicHonor is
  absent from every published page except the `.mmd`.
- All 18 pages: no What's New link (0 hits for "whats-new" in the four
  folders), and all four overviews open with `## Key Entities` straight after
  the H1 (H1 = 0).
- All four diagram pages are diagram-only or near-so (Finance 44 words,
  Recruiting 48, Survey 56; Student Academic Record 259 words, with prose only
  for the three subdomain diagrams).
- Descriptor tables are mostly inherited: 32 of 50 (Recruiting), 46 of 64
  (Survey), 19 of 37 (Finance), 66 of 89 (Student Academic Record) rows appear
  in 3 or more v6 domains (`table-dupes.csv`). Empty-cell ratios 0.31-0.39.
- Built-HTML meta description for overview and best-practices pages is just
  the first H2 text ("Key Entities", "Summary", "Current Grade Capture"), a
  concrete consequence of the known missing `description:` gap.
- Answerability across 32 questions: see the Answerability table below
  (tally at the end of that section).
