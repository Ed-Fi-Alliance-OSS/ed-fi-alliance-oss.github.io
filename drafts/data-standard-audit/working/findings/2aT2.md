# 2aT2: Terminology classification, v5 non-accepted hits

## Scope

- Source: `drafts/data-standard-audit/working/terminology-hits.csv`, rows where `set` = `v5` and `rule` is not `accepted`. That is 140 rows: `unlisted` 118, `bare` 21, `avoid` 1, `verb` 0.
- 25 v5 pages under `dataStandard_versioned_docs/version-5/` (model-reference, whats-new, readme.md, handbook.md).
- Rulebook: `drafts/data-standard-audit/terminology-guidelines.md`, including the Audit Rulings (Ed-Fi UDM accepted; Ed-Fi API Specification enforced).
- Output rows: `drafts/data-standard-audit/working/findings/2aT2.csv` (140 rows, one per hit).

## Method

- Opened every hit's source line in the v5 file. Where the regex matched at a line end, read the next line to see the full term. Most `bare` hits are line-wrap artifacts of this kind.
- Line numbers: the `line` values in `terminology-hits.csv` do not match the current source. Offsets range from -3 to +12 lines (for example, `readme.md` is off by 3). The CSV `line` column reports the actual source line, found by matching the hit's context text. Two hits on the same line (for example, rows 14 and 15) are kept as separate rows.
- Labels: `violation` breaks the guidelines; `acceptable` is a proper name, an accepted term split by a wrap, or a quoted title; `unsure` needs an owner's decision.
- `same_in_v6`: "yes (identical line)" means the same sentence appears verbatim in the same-path page under `version-6/`. "no v6 page" means no file at that path in version-6 (`whats-new/whats-new-v50.md`, `v51`, `v52`, and the `educator-preparation-data-model-domain` pages are absent there). "no" means the file exists but the sentence does not.
- Rubric G1 first-reference check: the first "Ed-Fi" on each page was matched against the accepted forms (Ed-Fi Unifying Data Model, Ed-Fi Data Standard, Ed-Fi Technology Suite, Ed-Fi ODS/API, Ed-Fi API Specification, Ed-Fi API, Ed-Fi ODS, Ed-Fi Alliance, Ed-Fi Community, Ed-Fi Data Handbook).
- Rewrite conventions used: data-model nouns become "Ed-Fi UDM" (after the first full reference) or "Ed-Fi Unifying Data Model (UDM)" (first use); standard concepts become "Ed-Fi Data Standard"; platform references become "Ed-Fi ODS/API"; organization actions become "Ed-Fi Alliance"; the community becomes "Ed-Fi Community" or "members of the Ed-Fi Community".

## Summary

- 140 hits: **122 violation**, **12 acceptable**, **6 unsure**.
- 24 of 25 pages have at least one violation. `whats-new/whats-new-v50.md` (24) and `whats-new/whats-new-v51.md` (13) and `whats-new-v52.md` (13) have the most.
- The largest group is "Ed-Fi" + data-model noun (47 hits: "Ed-Fi data model", "Ed-Fi model", "Ed-Fi core model"). The fix is "Ed-Fi UDM" after the first full reference.
- The lowercase community name ("Ed-Fi community", "Ed-Fi community members") accounts for 22 hits across five pages. The brief rules these violations, so capitalize to "Ed-Fi Community".
- The template sentence "Required attributes in Ed-Fi are hard constraints" appears on six domain pages (6 hits). All six lines are identical in v6, so one edit fixes both versions.
- Three avoid-list hits: "Ed-Fi technology team" (2, in `whats-new-v50.md` lines 171 and 279) and "Ed-Fi Standards" (1, in `readme.md` line 16).
- 20 of 25 pages have a first "Ed-Fi" reference that is not a full accepted form (G1). Listed below.
- Of the 122 violations, 68 have an identical line in v6 on 19 pages. 54 are on pages that do not exist in v6 (v5 release notes and the educator-preparation pages).
- Six unsure rows need an owner decision: the assessment specification name, the Data Exchange Standards link text, the SIG name, "Ed-Fi Governance", and two "Ed-Fi system" phrases.
- Out of scope but noticed: typos "theses" (`whats-new-v50.md` 249), "removal form" (260), and grammar "have marked" / "have shown" / "data model ... have" (several). Also `assessment-registration-domain/overview.md` line 52 says "Assessment domain" on a page for the Assessment Registration domain.
