# Agent 2b6 findings: Data Standard v6 model reference (4 domains)

AI-assisted: drafted by an AI agent; a person must review before use.

## Scope

Every v6 page, plus the `.mmd` diagram sources, under
`dataStandard_versioned_docs/version-6/model-reference/` for four domains:
`assessment-registration-domain`, `student-identification-and-demographics-domain`,
`student-health-domain`, and `intervention-domain` (19 pages: 4 readme, 4
overview, 4 model-diagrams, 4 entities, 3 best-practices; Intervention has no
best-practices page). Answerability covers the 32 questions in
`questions.yaml` whose `domain` is one of these four.

## Method

- Read each source page and the matching `.mmd` files, then checked
  `build/reference/data-exchange/data-standard/model-reference/<domain>/.../index.html`
  for `<title>`, meta description, rendered text, and image alt text.
- Used `inventory-html.csv`, `version-diff.csv`, `table-dupes.csv`,
  `terminology-hits.csv`, and `retrieval-results.csv` for metrics.
- Cross-checked entity names across the overview, the entities page, and both
  `.mmd` diagrams, and against `whats-new-v60.md`, `whats-new-v61.md`, and the v5
  What's New pages for answerability.
- Scoring: per rubric weights; page score is the weighted mean of applicable
  criteria. Criteria that apply by page type but have nothing to score on the
  page (R2 where there are no images, T1/T4 where there are no tables) are marked
  `n/a` and excluded. Where an overview begins directly with `## Key Entities`
  H1 = 0, per the rubric anchor; a first H2 of `## Purpose` with a good first
  paragraph scored 2.
- Site-wide issues (no `description`, no version in title/H1, Mermaid invisible,
  self-referencing canonicals, duplicated descriptor rows) are recorded once,
  and only with domain-specific additions.

## Summary

- Weighted page scores range 1.3 to 1.9 on the 0 to 3 scale; the weakest pages
  are the diagram pages (1.3 to 1.6) and the stale Assessment Registration and
  Student Identification best-practices pages.
- Two best-practices pages describe the v5 model, not v6: Assessment
  Registration (`sim_v6_v5` = 0.999) still lists Race, Sex, Address and
  StudentIdentificationCode on StudentEducationOrganization, and Student
  Identification still places StudentCharacteristic and Disability on
  StudentEducationOrganizationAssociation, which the v6 diagram puts on
  StudentDemographic.
- Student Health entities page has a copy-paste error: the StudentHealth entity
  is described as "actions taken by an education organization after a
  disruptive event that is recorded as a discipline incident".
- Vaccine codes are _not_ rows in the Student Health entities table. They are two
  untitled "Code / Short Description" tables appended after the descriptors
  table and the usage-classification tip. The rubric example (T5 = 0) overstates
  this; the real problems are the missing headings and a page title that says
  "Entities".
- FinancialAid moved into Student Identification and Demographics in v6.1, but
  its entities page still lists it under "Extended References" and its overview
  never mentions it. Two descriptor rows (AncestryEthnicOrigin,
  TelephoneNumberType) point at the wrong entity compared with the diagram.
- Intervention has no best-practices page, and nothing answers "Intervention vs
  Program". Its overview and readme are identical to v3, v4 and v5 (`sim` 1.0
  and 0.988), and retrieval returns the v3 page as top result for 3 questions.
- All four overviews have no outbound links (inventory `links` = 0) and three of
  four open directly with `## Key Entities`.
- Answerability: of 32 questions, 19 full, 12 partial, 1 none (see table).
  Learner vocabulary that never appears: "ethnicity", "Hispanic", "state ID",
  "district ID", "tutoring", "extended time", "vaccine codes" as a label.
- Strengths worth copying: the Assessment Registration use-case table (steps
  mapped to entities and reads/writes) and four-role table; Student Health
  overview Purpose and FERPA/HIPAA paragraph; Student Identification characteristic
  vs indicator guidance.
