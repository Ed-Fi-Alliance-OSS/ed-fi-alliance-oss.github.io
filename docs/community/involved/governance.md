# Governance

## Governed by the people who use it

As more states, districts, and vendors build on the Ed-Fi Data Standard, decisions about where it goes next can't rest on staff judgment alone. Governance is how the community's experience actually reaches the roadmap.

| Where changes start | How the standard keeps moving |
| -- | -- |
| Anyone in the Ed-Fi Community can propose a change to the data model or technology, grounded in what they're seeing in the field. | Proposals get prioritized, reviewed, and folded back into the roadmap on a regular cycle — not left to sit. |

## The Three Tiers

1. **Propose & debate** - Community groups made up of education agencies, vendors, and partners.
2. **Prioritize & poll** - Balancing voices across the ecosystem.
3. **Approve & advocate** - The Ed-Fi Alliance Management Committee.

### 2️⃣ Prioritize and Poll

The Governance Advisory Team and Technical Advisory Group are representative leaders from across the community. These leaders prioritize proposals, helping to balance voices across the ecosystem so changes meet the greatest community need.

- [Governance Advisory Team (GAT)](https://edfi.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GOV/pages/20317448/Governance+Advisory+Team)
- [Technical Advisory Group (TAG)](https://edfi.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GOV/pages/20317491/Technical+Advisory+Group)

## From Idea to Final Approval

Every change moves through the same stages, whether it starts with a work group or with Alliance staff.

1. **Idea initiation**: Someone raises a change or a gap in the standard.
2. **RFC process & engagement**: The idea becomes a request. The Ed-Fi Alliance team manages proposed changes, engaging with members of the community to design and validate them: [Data Standard Request for Comment](https://github.com/Ed-Fi-Alliance-OSS/Ed-Fi-Technology-Roadmap/discussions?discussions_q=label%3ARFC) • [Technology Suite Proposals](https://github.com/orgs/Ed-Fi-Alliance-OSS/projects/2/views/1)
3. **Incorporation of feedback**: Comments from both tracks get folded back into the proposal.
4. **TAG + GAT review**: Changes are reviewed by the Technical Advisory Group and Governance Advisory Team as needed.
5. **Final approval**: The Ed-Fi Alliance Management Committee signs off and the change is implemented on the roadmap.
